Values

Wis.ai is a personal assistant, built the way you would hire one in the real world.

When you start, you choose the characteristics you would value in a real assistant. Wis.ai gets to know you and what's important to you, and brings together everything it needs to be smart and helpful.

We're very capable of conjuring up fancy interfaces. We chose to focus on the three things that actually make an assistant valuable: intelligence, memory and a sense of humour.

The interface stays simple: whatever you already use on your phone every day. Wis.ai has its own computer and internet access, a voice, a number and an email address. It uses its devices the same way humans do.

We keep the complex technology out of your way. If you'd like to know more about what we believe in, read our values.

Wis.ai is currently available to a select private group while we build and scale our infrastructure.
